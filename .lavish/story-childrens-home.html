After sixteen years of renting, Humla's children have a permanent home

11 December 2024 · Simikot, Humla

RREADI Nepal
4 min read · Children

On 11 December 2024, the READI Children's Home in Simikot opened its doors in a building of its own: a permanent home for up to 30 children, built with support from the People of Japan.

Since READI was founded in 2008, the children's home has run in a rented building in Simikot. It gave children from across Humla a safe place to live, regular meals and the chance to go to school every day, but it was never a home that could be counted on for the long term.

Built to last

The building was funded through the Government of Japan's Grant Assistance for Grassroots Human Security Projects (GGP), a grant of USD 124,267 (about NPR 13.5 million). It can house up to 30 children and also serves as a training facility for the wider community.

"…improve the welfare of children in situations without their family protection…"Embassy of Japan in Nepal, on the handover

Chargé d'Affaires Tamura Takahiro attended the handover ceremony and congratulated everyone involved. The Embassy said it hopes the community will use and maintain the building well for many years.

What comes next

With a permanent home secured, the focus now turns to keeping it running: meals, school costs, health care and winter supplies for every child, every month. That is where monthly supporters make the biggest difference.
