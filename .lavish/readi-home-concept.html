Mission

Empowering people to transform their lives. READI is a volunteer-led Nepali NGO, founded by youth from Humla, working for education, nutrition, health and dignity for children and families across Karnali, and standing with communities when disaster strikes.

2008 · Where it began

The READI Children's Home, Simikot

A group of young people from Humla wanted local children to have a safe place to live and learn. They started in a rented building in Simikot, the hub of Nepal's most remote district.

December 2024 · A permanent home

A home built to last

After sixteen years of renting, the children moved into a home built to last. It has room for 30 children and doubles as a training centre for the whole community.

Partnership

From the People of Japan

The building was funded by the Government of Japan's Grassroots Human Security grant, USD 124,267, and handed over on 11 December 2024.

Today

Growing up in Humla, not far away

Children here go to school, eat well and stay close to their families and culture, instead of being sent to Kathmandu.
